## How to read the register

All entries were checked by the author on 2026-09-15; `reviewer=part_00_research`, `review_status=see_REVIEW.md`. `Confirmed` means that the claim is supported, within the stated scope, by a source that was read; it does not mean independent approval of the chapter. For source `P00-SNNN`, the snapshot is `P00-SNNN-20260915`; this is a conventional name for the access session, with no saved copy/hash. The `as_of` date is 2026-09-15 unless a historical period is given. All links and full provenance are in [Sources](Sources.md); direct URLs are repeated here for ease of checking.

The mathematical examples have no external empirical source: their conditions are set by the author, and the results are reproduced by arithmetic. A step from a definition to a design proposal is marked as an editorial recommendation, not a mandatory rule.

## Recheck triggers

Provider statuses/schemes — a change in documentation, API version or product terms. Legal definitions and interpretations — a new version/court judgment/reissue of the Q&A, and before any real-world application. Empirical indicators — a new observation period or a change in method. Money examples — any change to the initial conditions, fee/refund model, currency or recognition timing. An independent reviewer must recheck material locators and arithmetic without relying on the author's self-check.
