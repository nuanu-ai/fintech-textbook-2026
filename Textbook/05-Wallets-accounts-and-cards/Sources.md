## Fields common to all cards

- `accessed_at=2026-09-15, Asia/Makassar (UTC+08:00)`; `date_precision=day` for reading. The date on which a search engine returned or indexed a page must not be taken as the publication date.
- `snapshot_id=<source_id>-20260915`; `supersedes=none`. The IDs date the observation; this is **not** a claim about a saved copy, archive, commit or immutability.
- `local_path=not_saved`, `sha256=not_computed`; PDFs/illustrations are not included in the deliverable. The text is original, with short attributed paraphrases.
- `origin_source_id=source_id` for each primary document; `locale` is the jurisdiction in Scope, with no assumption about availability to the user. `language=EN`; `doi=not_available` unless given. The canonical URL is the same as the URL given unless stated otherwise.
- `effective_from/to=not_applicable` for non-normative documents unless stated otherwise. For the normative basis, the date is unknown where it was not checked, rather than filled in from memory.
- `rights`: links and limited educational paraphrase; permission for full copying, images, full translation and external distribution is **unknown** unless the card explicitly states a license. This is not a claim that rights to third-party material have been granted. `rights_basis_url` is the source itself/its visible license; no separate license review was carried out.
- `reviewer=see_REVIEW.md`, `reviewed_at=see_REVIEW.md`, `review_status=see_REVIEW.md`. The author's check is not an independent review.
- Sources from the same publisher belong to one `independence_group` (EBA/EC, FDIC, FCA, Coinbase, Stripe, Marqeta, Visa, etc.). One provider's documentation, marketing and contract are not counted as independent confirmation of its effectiveness. The research paper and Reddit are separate groups.

`full_text` means the substantive text is available; actual reading is limited to the stated `reviewed_scope`. Partial reading of a PDF is stated explicitly. `search_index` means reading an available representation of the original through an index after a direct fetch failed, not circumventing restrictions.
