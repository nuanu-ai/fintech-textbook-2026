## How to read the source cards

Each card covers the material actually read, as stated in `reviewed_scope`. `full_text` for a short HTML article means the text of that article, not the whole site; `partial_text` for a PDF means the named pages. Removed pages and search candidates are moved to [RESEARCH_NOTES.md](Research-notes.md). Sources from the same `independence_group` do not count as independent voices.

**Common fields for every card below:** `accessed_at=2026-09-15 UTC`; access precision is the calendar day (the clock was checked with a tool during the work); publication `date_precision` is the day if a full day is given, otherwise explicitly month/unknown; `language=en`, `locale=international/stated jurisdiction`; `canonical_url=url` unless a republication is noted; `doi=unknown`; `supersedes=none in this local registry`; `effective_to=unknown` for legal/contractual documents, `not_applicable` for the rest. `local_path=none`, `sha256=none`: the source files were not saved. The snapshot ID marks the reading date, **not an immutable copy**. PDFs were processed in memory.

**Rights:** links and a brief original synthesis; permission to store full copies, use illustrations and redistribute is `unknown`, and none of these was done. `rights_basis_url` defaults to the URL of the document itself; no separate license was established; unknown does not mean permission. ERC-681 explicitly states CC0; CPMI147 permits brief attributed excerpts/translation, and no permission for any other use is claimed.

**Card review:** `reviewer=part_06_research (author)`, `reviewed_at=2026-09-15 UTC`, independent `review_status=see_REVIEW.md`. Reading and cross-checking by the author is not an independent review. Vendor sources have an interest in describing and promoting their own services; regulators, in their own policies; specific limitations are noted in the cards.
