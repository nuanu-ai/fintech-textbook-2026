## How to read the register

One row is a specific version of a document that was read, not an open-ended endorsement of the website. Fields common to all source cards: `snapshot_id = <source_id>-20260915`; `accessed_at=2026-09-15 Asia/Makassar`, precision day; `supersedes=none`; `doi=not_applicable`; `canonical_url=url` unless noted otherwise; `effective_to=unknown` unless stated; `local_path=none`, `sha256=none` (immutable copies were not saved); `rights: quotation=limited necessary paraphrase/short quote; storage/redistribution/illustrations=not_assessed`; `rights_basis_url=unknown`. The author and publisher is the named organization unless stated otherwise. `origin_source_id=self`, `independence_group=publisher`: different pages of the same regulator are not independent confirmations. `reviewer=part_01_research` means a reading by the author, not an independent review; `reviewed_at=2026-09-15`, independent reviewer: see REVIEW.md. Unknown dates are marked unknown; indexing dates are not used as document dates.

`full_text` means that the text was available, not that every line of a large act was read: what was actually read is listed in scope. `partial_text/search_index` is not called a direct reading of the original and is not used as the sole support for a categorical legal claim. The material needs updating before release: dynamic pages can change their content at the same link.

## Gaps and rejected shortcuts

- MAS PSA 2019 on SSO returned 403; the main MAS site was under maintenance. The official SGPC PDFs S011–S012 were read directly. A MAS announcement of 2026-09-01 about a consultation on legislative amendments to the stablecoin framework was found; the original was unavailable, and the PublicNow reprint did not open either. **Not included as confirmed law**; the operative 2026 regime must not be inferred from the 2023 finalized framework announcement.
- EUR-Lex showed a JS challenge for the PSD2/MiCA EN HTML/PDF and for an alternative locale; instead of bypassing it, the official BOE was used. We do not claim that the BOE original replaces the current national consolidation.
- The US GENIUS final-rule trigger requires a full reconciliation of regulatory releases; the OCC proposal found does not settle this question. The reference to the law confirms the condition, not the fact that it has been triggered.
- The Reddit PCI thread `https://www.reddit.com/r/pcicompliance/comments/1j0g7w1/` (index: 2025-02-28) did not open; author/context not confirmed. It is not used as an attributed case. X yielded no substantive accessible primary material for the claims; a quota of social links is not simulated.
- Academic travel-rule papers found only as abstracts/metadata were not used as research that had been read. The teaching diagrams and calculations are the author's own, not empirical results of these papers.
- This is the author's evidence register, not a report on checking all firm registers or all national amendments. No due diligence of any specific partner was carried out.
