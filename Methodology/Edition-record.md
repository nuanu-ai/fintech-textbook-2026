# Public edition record

Public teaching edition of **2026-09-18**. Knowledge cutoff: 2026-09-15; the original detailed fact-check was completed on 2026-09-17. The materials keep the status `reviewed_with_limitations`: the repository being available does not make every claim fully proven.

The 10 chapters, 65 modules, worked calculations, exercises and answers, 471 permanent claim IDs and 353 permanent source IDs are preserved. 352 substantive cards are published; P09-S021 is an explicit record of an excluded internal source. The check of the move to the public edition covers meaning, privacy, links and file correspondence, not a repeat fact-check of all external websites as of 18 September.

<a id="public-adaptations"></a>
## Changes in the public version

1. Internal organizational links, internal service identifiers and the instruction for handing over internal context were removed. Organizational documents and the history of other repositories are not included.
2. The personal open check P05-C044 was generalized to refer to a specific user; its `open` / `unverifiable` were not upgraded. Names of internal environments in the description of the course's limits were replaced with general categories.
3. P09-S021 was excluded, with its ID preserved. P09-C008 and P09-C009 were replaced with editorial statements explicitly attributed to the authors. Their rating in the public matrices is `non_factual`; the previous `verified` does not carry over to the new wording. Hypotheses H1–H3 remain hypotheses.
4. Hence the public totals: **296 verified, 78 verified_as_reported, 24 partial, 21 unverifiable, 38 non_factual, 14 contradicted**. The original audit of 17 September had 298 verified and 36 non_factual. All other ratings are preserved.
5. Other external sources, URLs, access limitations, methodological conclusions, amounts and solutions to the exercises are preserved. Public links are not treated as permission to republish third-party PDFs or figures.

## Reader-friendly layout · 18 September 2026

Chapter folders and reference files were given Russian names. Chapters open as soon as you enter the folder; a cover, a table of contents of 65 lessons, navigation between chapters and a separate section for sources and fact-checking were added. Lesson titles were rendered into plain Russian, keeping their numbers and previous anchors. The technical headers of the manuscripts and two outdated closing notes about awaiting review were moved into collapsible blocks at the end of the chapters. Old addresses of the form `chapters/part-XX/chapter.md` were replaced: for new bookmarks use the [table of contents](../Textbook/README.md).

The teaching text, calculations, external links, 471 claim IDs, 353 source IDs and the fact-check results are preserved. This is editorial layout, not a new fact-check. The historical plan was not rewritten: it shows the design of the original research; the current [table of contents](../Textbook/README.md) reflects the published lessons.

## English edition · 22 October 2026

The textbook was translated from Russian into English. The Russian edition of 18 September 2026 is kept unchanged under the tag [`ru-final`](https://github.com/nuanu-ai/fintech-textbook-2026/tree/ru-final). Folder and file names, chapter and lesson titles are now in English.

1. **What was kept.** All 10 chapters and 65 lessons, worked calculations, exercises and answers; 471 claim IDs and 353 source IDs; every URL; all fact-check verdicts (296 verified, 78 verified_as_reported, 24 partial, 21 unverifiable, 38 non_factual, 14 contradicted); explicit anchors such as `module-6-1`.
2. **How it was translated.** Agents translated the chapters in parallel with one shared glossary. A separate agent reviewed every chapter against the Russian original, paragraph by paragraph. Automatic checks confirmed that claim and source IDs, URLs, explicit anchors, code blocks and the line structure of every file match the Russian files, that numbers and dates match after format conversion, and that every internal link and anchor resolves.
3. **Formats.** Numeric dates are written as `YYYY-MM-DD`; numbers use `1,000.00`. References to chapters say "Chapter N"; Parts I–III group the chapters on the cover and in the contents. The original plan keeps its own historical terms.
4. **What this is not.** The translation is not a new fact-check and not an update of laws or products: the knowledge cutoff stays 15 September 2026. Small inconsistencies found in the original during translation, such as different page locators for the same source in a register and in its audit, were left unchanged and recorded for a later fact-check.
5. **Old addresses.** Russian folder and file names no longer exist on `main`; use the [contents](../Textbook/README.md) or the `ru-final` tag.
