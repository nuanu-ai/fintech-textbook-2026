# Week 43 · 19–25 October 2026

Events dated 2026-10-19 … 2026-10-25 by their primary source. Researched 2026-11-09 and checked against primary sources in a separate pass on 2026-11-10. Teaching edition with limitations — not legal advice.

## Highlights

- The OCC published its final GENIUS Act rule for issuers under its supervision. The rule takes effect on 18 January 2027.
- Canada published draft stablecoin regulations that would require issuers to register with the Bank of Canada and redeem within three business days.
- The UK Payment Systems Regulator removed the expiry date of its Confirmation of Payee direction.
- Visa, BILL and Billtrust announced a live pilot for AI agents to complete buyer-approved invoice payments.
