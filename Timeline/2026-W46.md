# Week 46 · 9–15 November 2026

Events dated 2026-11-09 … 2026-11-15 by their primary source. Researched and checked against primary sources in separate passes on 2026-11-16.

## Highlights

- Canada launched the Real-Time Rail for instant settlement around the clock, with mandatory centralised fraud services at launch.
- FinCEN and the Federal Reserve withdrew a proposal to lower the threshold for specified cross-border funds-transfer records from $3,000 to $250.
- Kenya’s central bank and capital markets regulator directed virtual-asset service providers to apply for licences under the country’s new regulatory framework.
- Travelport and its partners demonstrated an AI agent paying for and ticketing a live flight with a tokenised Mastercard credential.

## EU and UK

- **2026-11-09 · SEPA · payment scheme rulebooks · published.** The European Payments Council published change proposals for its five payment schemes, including SEPA Credit Transfer and SEPA Instant Credit Transfer, alongside stakeholder positions. This is a record of proposals in the rulebook revision process. [EPC — 2026 payment scheme rulebook change proposals and stakeholder positions published, 2026-11-09](https://www.europeanpaymentscouncil.eu/news-insights/news/2026-payment-scheme-rulebook-change-proposals-and-stakeholder-positions) → [lesson 2.3](../Textbook/02-How-a-payment-works/README.md#module-2-3)

## United States and Canada

- **2026-11-09 · Canada · Real-Time Rail · launched.** Payments Canada launched a national system that lets participating financial institutions and payment service providers exchange, clear and settle payments in real time around the clock. It uses ISO 20022 and mandatory centralised fraud services at launch. [Payments Canada — Canada launches the Real-Time Rail, bringing 24/7 payment infrastructure to the national economy, 2026-11-09](https://www.payments.ca/canada-launches-real-time-rail-bringing-247-payment-infrastructure-national-economy) → [lesson 2.3](../Textbook/02-How-a-payment-works/README.md#module-2-3)
- **2026-11-09 · Canada · Real-Time Rail oversight · published.** The Bank of Canada announced that it had designated the Real-Time Rail a prominent payment system under the Payment Clearing and Settlement Act, effective 2026-11-08. The designation puts the operator under the Bank’s risk-management oversight. [Bank of Canada — Bank of Canada designates RTR as a prominent payment system, 2026-11-09](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/2026/11/bank-of-canada-designates-rtr-as-a-prominent-payment-system/)
- **2026-11-09 · United States · cross-border transfer recordkeeping and Travel Rule proposal · withdrawal published.** FinCEN and the Federal Reserve withdrew their 2020 proposal to lower the threshold for specified cross-border transfer records from USD 3,000 to USD 250. The withdrawal also covers the proposed corresponding change to FinCEN’s Travel Rule threshold and the proposed clarification of how the rules apply to certain digital assets. [GovInfo — Federal Register notice 2026-22809, 2026-11-09](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-09/html/2026-22809.htm) → [lesson 1.5](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-5)
- **2026-11-10 · Canada · Iran-related sanctions · published.** Global Affairs Canada announced the reimposition of sanctions on 11 Iranian entities that Canada had delisted in 2016. Canadian persons and businesses are subject to the property and transaction restrictions that apply to listed entities. [Global Affairs Canada — Minister Anand announces reimposition of sanctions against Iranian entities, 2026-11-10](https://www.canada.ca/en/global-affairs/news/2026/11/minister-anand-announces-reimposition-of-sanctions-against-iranian-entities.html) → [lesson 1.5](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-5)
- **2026-11-12 · United States · Federal Reserve payment service pricing · published.** The Federal Reserve Board announced 2027 pricing effective 2027-01-01 across services including ACH, instant payments and wholesale settlement. For FedNow, a new incentive programme replaces the discount that waived per-item fees on the first 2,500 transactions per month. [Federal Reserve — 2027 pricing announcement, 2026-11-12](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/other20261112a.htm) · [Federal Reserve — pricing notice, FedNow section](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/files/other20261112a2.pdf) → [lesson 2.3](../Textbook/02-How-a-payment-works/README.md#module-2-3)
- **2026-11-12 · United States (New York) · virtual-currency advertising · published.** New York’s Department of Financial Services set out its expectations for advertising and marketing by regulated virtual-currency entities, including evidence for claims and board or designated committee approval of policies. Firms must have compliant policies available for review from 2027-05-12. [NYDFS — Guidance on Advertising and Marketing, 2026-11-12](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/industry-guidance/industry-letters/il20261110-guidance-on-advertising-and-marketing) → [lesson 6.5](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-5)

## Global networks and AI-agent payments

- **2026-11-10 · Global · Visa stablecoin settlement on Monad · published.** The Monad Foundation announced that participating Visa issuers and acquirers can use Monad as another network for settling Visa obligations in stablecoins. The announcement identifies no first institution settling on Monad. [Monad Foundation — Monad Joins Visa’s Stablecoin Settlement Program, 2026-11-10](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/10/3399913/0/en/monad-joins-visa-s-stablecoin-settlement-program.html) → [lesson 6.3](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-3)
- **2026-11-12 · Global · agent-initiated flight payment · live demonstration announced.** Travelport, Booked AI, Basis Theory and Mastercard reported that an AI agent completed a live flight booking, including payment and ticketing. The customer approved the booking and amount, Mastercard Agent Pay used a tokenised card credential, and the airline remained the merchant. [Mastercard — Travelport, Booked AI, Basis Theory, and Mastercard complete live end-to-end agentic flight purchase, 2026-11-12](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/press/2026/november/end-to-end-AI-powered-travel-booking.html) → [lesson 7.2](../Textbook/07-AI-agent-payments/README.md#module-7-2)
