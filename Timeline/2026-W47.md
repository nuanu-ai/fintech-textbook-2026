# Week 47 · 16–22 November 2026

Events dated 2026-11-16 … 2026-11-22 by their primary source or scheduled application date. Researched and checked against primary sources in separate passes on 2026-11-23. Teaching edition with limitations.

## Highlights

- MAS published guidance on the legal treatment of tokenised bank deposits and a roadmap for PayNow enhancements over 2027–2028.
- The OCC gave preliminary conditional approval to Payoneer’s proposed national trust bank, whose plans include a closed-loop stablecoin.
- Singapore and Germany reported a test of cross-currency settlement between independent central bank test environments.
- Russia’s cryptocurrency exchange trading ordinance reached its scheduled application date, with each exchange still needing concurrence on its trading rules.

## Asia-Pacific

- **2026-11-16 · Singapore and Germany · Project Simbolon cross-currency settlement · published (test results).** MAS and the Deutsche Bundesbank reported successful EUR/SGD payment-versus-payment tests using wholesale CBDC in independent central bank test environments. Cryptographic proofs let the environments verify settlement conditions without direct integration between their ledgers. [MAS — MAS and Deutsche Bundesbank Advance Cross-Border Digital Asset Settlement Connectivity, 2026-11-16](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/mas-and-deutsche-bundesbank-advance-cross-border-digital-asset-settlement-connectivity) → [lesson 6.8](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-8)
- **2026-11-16 · Hong Kong · Project Ensemble and Swift ledger · published (exploration).** HKMA announced work with Swift and participating banks to explore interoperability between the Swift ledger and Project Ensemble’s settlement layer. The work examines tokenised deposits in cross-border payments. [HKMA — HKMA and Swift continue the collaboration to explore cross-border payment interoperability through Project Ensemble and Swift ledger, 2026-11-16](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/11/20261116-4/)
- **2026-11-18 · Singapore · PayNow Generation 2 · published (implementation roadmap).** MAS and the Association of Banks in Singapore set out staged enhancements for 2027–2028, including QR interoperability, deep-linking and richer payment data. The roadmap also plans clearer rules for disputes, refunds, fraud and scams. [MAS and ABS — MAS and ABS Set Out PayNow Generation 2 Implementation Roadmap, 2026-11-18](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/mas-and-abs-set-out-paynow-generation-2-implementation-roadmap) → [lesson 2.3](../Textbook/02-How-a-payment-works/README.md#module-2-3)
- **2026-11-18 · Singapore · tokenised bank deposits · published (guidance).** MAS issued guidance applying existing deposit definitions to tokenised arrangements. Classification depends on the parties’ legal and economic rights and obligations, with corresponding treatment under deposit insurance, insolvency and resolution frameworks. [MAS — Guide on the Tokenisation of Bank Deposits, 2026-11-18](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/regulation/guidelines/ppd/guide-on-the-tokenisation-of-bank-deposits/guide-on-the-tokenisation-of-bank-deposits_18-nov-2026vf.pdf) → [lesson 1.4](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-4)
- **2026-11-18 · Singapore and Asia · institutional USDC access through DBS · published (service announcement).** DBS and Circle announced direct USDC minting and redemption through DBS for eligible institutional clients. The arrangement supports 24/7 USD–USDC settlement. [DBS — DBS and Circle expand institutional access to USDC in Asia, 2026-11-18](https://www.dbs.com/newsroom/DBS_and_Circle_expand_institutional_access_to_USDC_in_Asia) → [lesson 6.3](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-3)
- **2026-11-19 · Singapore · tokenised MAS Bills · published (trial results).** MAS confirmed a S$100 million four-week tokenised bill issue on 2026-11-06, settled in wholesale CBDC on its Singapore Dollar Test Network. It intends to expand issuance, participation and collateral-management uses in further trials. [MAS — MAS Announces Successful Issuance of Tokenised MAS Bills on the Singapore Dollar Test Network Using Wholesale Central Bank Digital Currency, 2026-11-19](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/mas-announces-successful-issuance-of-tokenised-mas-bills-on-the-singapore-dollar-test-network) → [lesson 6.8](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-8)
- **2026-11-19 · Singapore · interoperability of tokenised bank deposits · published (proof of concept).** StraitsX, OCBC and Standard Chartered reported completing USD payment flows between the banks’ tokenised deposit ecosystems outside conventional banking cutoffs. Wider implementation remains subject to regulatory, legal and operational arrangements. [StraitsX — StraitsX, OCBC and Standard Chartered Explore Interoperability Between Tokenised Deposits Networks, 2026-11-19](https://www.straitsx.com/blog-post/straitsx-ocbc-and-standard-chartered-explore-interoperability-between-tokenised-deposits-networks)
- **2026-11-19 · Singapore · digital money settlement arrangements · published (field guide).** BLOOM participants published a framework for analysing digital money arrangements through functions, structure, connectivity and controls. The guide supplies implementation reference points for institutions connecting different forms of money. [MAS — BLOOM: A Field Guide to Extending Settlement Capabilities, version 1.0, 2026-11-19](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/development/fintech/bloom/bloom_field_guide.pdf)
