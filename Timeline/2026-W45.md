# Week 45 · 2–8 November 2026

Events dated 2026-11-02 … 2026-11-08 by their primary source. Researched 2026-11-09; every entry was checked against its primary source by a second agent. Teaching edition with limitations — not legal advice.

## Highlights

- Nigeria's central bank issued regulations that license stablecoin issuers and other non-security virtual asset providers, in force from 2026-11-05.
- In the West African Economic and Monetary Union, interoperable e-money transfers between individuals had to go through the PI-SPI instant payment platform from 2026-11-02.
- The US Financial Crimes Enforcement Network issued an order against the crypto exchange MEXC Global, and the US Treasury sanctioned eight Canadian money services businesses over Iran-linked flows.
- The Council's first-reading position on the EU payment services directive and regulation (PSD3 and PSR) was published, and the Parliament's second reading is indicatively planned for 2026-12-14.

## Latin America

- **2026-11-05 · Argentina · interoperability of instant-transfer schemes · adopted (staged dates in 2027).** Communication "A" 8508 of the central bank (BCRA) requires every instant-transfer scheme to interoperate with the others, so that participants can send to any other account provider at the same service level as an in-scheme transfer, even if the schemes' rules differ. The rules will sit in a standardised payments interface whose definitions and service-level agreement the BCRA has yet to set after a non-binding consultation; the low-value clearing house must give scheme administrators replicas of the database of payment-account virtual keys (CVUs) from 2027-02-15 and an online interface from 2027-03-31, and from 2027-04-06 the administrators of the Red Link, Newpay and COELSA schemes must implement interoperable operating flows. The wording "DEBIN" and "CREDIN" may no longer be used in customer interfaces, and the cover text gives no start date for the main interoperability rule.
