# Week 38 · 15–20 September 2026

Events dated 2026-09-15 … 2026-09-20 by their primary source. Researched 2026-10-22; every entry was checked against its primary source by a second agent. Teaching edition with limitations — not legal advice.

## Highlights

- The European Central Bank (ECB) opened a call for online and mobile merchants to join a digital euro pilot expected to start in the second half of 2027.
- The UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) published final guidance on which cryptoasset activities need its authorization under the regime that starts on 2027-10-25.
- Brazil's central bank amended the Pix rules so that a provider that marks a customer's tax ID for suspected fraud must block that customer's Pix key requests and transactions.
- The National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) published a framework for charging merchants a fee on some Unified Payments Interface (UPI) payments, ending zero-fee pricing for them.
- Saudi Arabia's mada and Qatar's Himyan national card schemes began, gradually, to accept each other's cards.

## EU and UK

- **2026-09-15 · EU · digital euro · pilot call opened.** The ECB invited euro-area online and mobile merchants to join a digital euro pilot for payments in remote commerce, with applications due 2026-10-27. The pilot is expected to start in the second half of 2027, last 12 months and use a beta digital euro without legal tender status; the decision on issuance still comes only after the EU legislation is adopted. [ECB — Call for online and mobile merchants to participate in digital euro pilot now open, 2026-09-15](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/intro/news/html/ecb.mipnews260915.en.html) → [lesson 6.8](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-8)
- **2026-09-16 · United Kingdom · crypto regulation · final guidance published.** The FCA published final guidance (PS26/18) on when issuing qualifying stablecoins, running trading platforms, dealing, arranging, safeguarding and arranging staking need FCA authorization; existing registrations under the Money Laundering Regulations will not convert automatically. Firms seeking transitional arrangements can apply from 2026-09-30 to 2027-02-28, and the regime starts on 2027-10-25. [FCA — PS26/18: Cryptoasset perimeter guidance, 2026-09-16](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/policy-statements/ps26-18-cryptoasset-perimeter-guidance); [FCA — Crypto firms get guidance on how the new regime applies, 2026-09-16](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/crypto-firms-get-guidance-how-new-regime-applies) → [lesson 1.4](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-4), [lesson 6.5](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-5)
- **2026-09-20 · EU · Verification Of Payee scheme · rulebook applies from.** Version 1.1 of the European Payments Council (EPC) Verification Of Payee (VOP) scheme rulebook, issued on 2026-03-16, became effective on 2026-09-20. VOP is the check that matches the payee name entered by the payer against the account before a transfer; what version 1.1 changes was not checked. [EPC — Verification Of Payee Scheme Rulebook, EPC218-23 2026 Version 1.1, 2026-03-16](https://www.europeanpaymentscouncil.eu/sites/default/files/kb/file/2026-03/EPC218-23%20v1.1%202026%20Verification%20Of%20Payee%20Scheme%20Rulebook_0.pdf) → [lesson 2.3](../Textbook/02-How-a-payment-works/README.md#module-2-3)

## United States and Canada

- **2026-09-15 · United States · crypto market-structure bill · Senate procedural vote failed.** The Senate did not invoke cloture on the motion to proceed to H.R. 3633, the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act (CLARITY Act), by 49 votes to 50 (Record Vote No. 234), and a motion to reconsider was entered. The House passed the bill in July 2025; no further Senate step was recorded through 2026-10-22, so there is still no federal statute on crypto market structure. [congress.gov — H.R. 3633 (119th Congress): all actions, 2026-09-15](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/3633/all-actions) → [lesson 1.4](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-4)

## Latin America

- **2026-09-18 · Brazil · Pix rules and fraud controls · adopted.** The Central Bank of Brazil (BCB) amended the Pix instant payment regulation with Resolution 587: among other changes, a provider can mark a customer's tax ID (CPF/CNPJ) for suspected fraud, and that provider must then refuse the customer's requests to register, port or claim Pix keys and reject Pix transactions involving the customer except returns; from 2027-02-01 it must also tell the customer about the marking. The resolution applies from its publication on 2026-09-21, except provisions deferred to 2027-02-01 (including a "hybrid" Pix charge payable through the boleto bank-slip system) and 2027-07-01; Normative Instruction 777 of the same day publishes version 2.2 of the Pix flows manual, applying from 2027-02-01. [BCB — Resolução BCB nº 587, de 18 de setembro de 2026 (BCB Resolution No. 587 of 18 September 2026), 2026-09-18](https://www.bcb.gov.br/api/conteudo/app/normativos/exibenormativo?p1=Resolu%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20BCB&p2=587); [BCB — Instrução Normativa BCB nº 777, de 18 de setembro de 2026 (BCB Normative Instruction No. 777), 2026-09-18](https://www.bcb.gov.br/api/conteudo/app/normativos/exibenormativo?p1=Instru%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Normativa%20BCB&p2=777) → [lesson 2.3](../Textbook/02-How-a-payment-works/README.md#module-2-3), [lesson 2.8](../Textbook/02-How-a-payment-works/README.md#module-2-8)

## Asia-Pacific

- **2026-09-15 · India · UPI merchant pricing · framework published.** NPCI published FAQs announcing a merchant discount rate (MDR), a fee paid by the merchant, on select person-to-merchant UPI payments, ending zero-fee pricing for them. In the FAQ as updated on 2026-10-15 the rate is 0.4% on such payments above ₹2,000, consumers pay nothing, person-to-person payments stay free and small merchants stay at zero; on 2026-10-13 NPCI said the fee will start after Diwali on a date to be notified. [NPCI — FAQs – Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) on Select UPI (P2M) Transactions, Updated 15 October 2026, 2026-10-15](https://www.npci.org.in/uploads/FA_Qs_Merchant_Discount_Rate_MDR_on_Select_UPI_P2_M_Transactions_Updated_15_October_2026_a1f433513f.pdf); [NPCI — UPI MDR on Select Merchant Transactions Shall be Implemented Post Diwali, 2026-10-13](https://www.npci.org.in/uploads/UPI_MDR_on_Select_Merchant_Transactions_Shall_be_Implemented_Post_Diwali_4c9cdd77d5.pdf) → [lesson 2.4](../Textbook/02-How-a-payment-works/README.md#module-2-4), [lesson 2.8](../Textbook/02-How-a-payment-works/README.md#module-2-8)

## Middle East and Türkiye

- **2026-09-15 · Saudi Arabia, Qatar · domestic card schemes · cross-border acceptance launched (gradual).** The Saudi Central Bank (SAMA), in cooperation with Qatar Central Bank, announced the gradual start of acceptance of Saudi "mada" national cards in Qatar and of Qatar's national "Himyan" card in Saudi Arabia, as part of Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) payments integration. It is an example of two national schemes accepting each other's cards across a border; merchant coverage, rollout dates and fees were not given. [SAMA — Saudi Central Bank announces start of acceptance of national payment cards "mada" in Qatar and "Himyan" in Saudi Arabia (translated from Arabic), 2026-09-15](https://www.sama.gov.sa/ar-sa/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-1168.aspx) → [lesson 3.4](../Textbook/03-Visa-and-Mastercard-cards/README.md#module-3-4)

## Africa

- **2026-09-16 · West African Economic and Monetary Union (WAEMU) · instant payments · business APIs approved.** The Central Bank of West African States (BCEAO) approved the business application programming interfaces (APIs) of participants in PI-SPI, the union's interoperable instant payment platform, so that firms can send and receive payments, submit bulk payments and track them in real time across the union, whichever provider each side uses. The annexed list names 24 approved APIs, including Ecobank's in all eight member states; it counts approvals, not live corporate use. [BCEAO — « Homologation des API Business de la Plateforme Interopérable du Système de Paiement Instantané (PI-SPI) de l'UEMOA » (Approval of the PI-SPI Business APIs), 2026-09-16](https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/homologation-des-api-business-de-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de) → [lesson 2.3](../Textbook/02-How-a-payment-works/README.md#module-2-3)

## Russia and CIS

- **2026-09-18 · Russia · bank exposure to crypto · draft regulation (consultation).** The Bank of Russia published for impact assessment a draft regulation with two new bank ratios, N31 (solo) and N32 (consolidated), each with a 1% limit on risk from investments in digital currencies and foreign digital instruments, counting both direct holdings and instruments whose payouts depend on crypto prices. The rules would take effect 10 days after official publication, which the Bank expects in the fourth quarter of 2026. [Банк России — «Банк России введет новые банковские нормативы для ограничения рисков вложений в криптовалюты» (Bank of Russia will introduce new bank ratios to limit risks of investing in cryptocurrencies), 2026-09-18](https://www.cbr.ru/press/event/?id=32856) → [lesson 6.5](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-5)
