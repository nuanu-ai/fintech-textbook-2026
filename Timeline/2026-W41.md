# Week 41 · 5–11 October 2026

Events dated 2026-10-05 … 2026-10-11 by their primary source. Researched 2026-10-22; every entry was checked against its primary source by a second agent. Teaching edition with limitations — not legal advice.

## Highlights

- The European Securities and Markets Authority told EU-authorized crypto firms to stop serving EU clients in stablecoins that do not comply with the EU crypto regulation.
- The US Financial Crimes Enforcement Network withdrew its 2020 proposal on unhosted crypto wallets and its 2023 proposal on crypto mixing.
- The UK sanctioned the owner of the Cryptomus crypto payment gateway and Kyrgyz payment processors under its Russia regime.
- The Bank of Russia admitted the first five digital depositories and four crypto exchangers to its new registers.
- The Monetary Authority of Singapore issued final AI risk management guidelines for financial institutions that address agentic AI.

## EU and UK

- **2026-10-08 · EU · stablecoins under MiCA · opinion published.** The European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA) said that crypto-asset service providers (CASPs) authorized under the Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA) "should cease providing services related to non-MiCA-compliant stablecoins to clients in the European Union" and should stop clients from acquiring or increasing such exposure. National authorities should require existing exposures to be remediated within three months of publication; continued service is allowed only when strictly limited to liquidation, conversion, withdrawal, transfer or safekeeping. [ESMA — ESMA sets out supervisory expectations on services related to unauthorised stablecoins, 2026-10-08](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-sets-out-supervisory-expectations-services-related-unauthorised) → [lesson 6.5](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-5), [lesson 1.4](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-4)
- **2026-10-08 · United Kingdom · sanctions on crypto and payment firms · sanctions.** Under its Russia sanctions regime, the UK added 38 entries, including Xeltox Enterprises (designated through its ownership of Cryptomus and activities continued via Heleket), the Kyrgyz firms TokenSpot, Tsunami Payments and Processing KG, and the Russian bank Stolichny Kredit. The crypto and payment firms face an asset freeze, a ban on correspondent banking and payment processing, and internet services sanctions that require app stores and internet providers to take reasonable steps to block UK access. [Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office — Sanctions Notice: Russia, 8 October 2026, 2026-10-08](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6ac767c6e8ef5609e7c93369/Sanctions_Notice__Russia__8_October_2026.pdf) → [lesson 1.5](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-5)

## United States and Canada

- **2026-10-05 · United States · crypto anti-money laundering proposals · withdrawn.** The Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) withdrew its 2020 proposal that would have required banks and money services businesses to report, keep records and verify identity for crypto transactions involving unhosted wallets, and its 2023 proposal to treat international crypto mixing as a primary money-laundering concern; both withdrawals took effect on 2026-10-06. FinCEN presents this as part of a "deregulatory agenda"; the notices only withdraw proposals and do not say that existing recordkeeping or travel rule obligations changed. [FinCEN — FinCEN Announces Withdrawals of Proposed Digital Asset Related Rules, 2026-10-05](https://www.fincen.gov/news/news-releases/fincen-announces-withdrawals-proposed-digital-asset-related-rules); [Federal Register 2026-20430 — Requirements for Certain Transactions Involving Convertible Virtual Currency or Digital Assets; Withdrawal, 2026-10-06](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-10-06/html/2026-20430.htm); [Federal Register 2026-20429 — Proposal of Special Measure Regarding Convertible Virtual Currency Mixing as a Class of Transactions…; Withdrawal, 2026-10-06](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/06/2026-20429/proposal-of-special-measure-regarding-convertible-virtual-currency-mixing-as-a-class-of-transactions) → [lesson 1.5](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-5), [lesson 6.5](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-5)
- **2026-10-08 · United States · anti-money laundering enforcement against a card issuer · enforcement.** The Federal Reserve Board announced an enforcement action against American Express Company for failing to adequately detect and report suspicious activity tied to money laundering and for significant weaknesses in its enterprise-wide anti-money laundering program, particularly at its national bank subsidiary; the release says the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) issued a separate action against the bank. The OCC's own page, including any penalty amount, could not be opened. [Federal Reserve Board — Federal Reserve Board announces enforcement action against American Express Company…, 2026-10-08](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/enforcement20261008a.htm) → [lesson 1.5](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-5)

## Asia-Pacific

- **2026-10-05 · Indonesia · crypto trader licensing · transfer of applications completed.** Indonesia's Financial Services Authority (OJK) reported that licensing PT Gudang Kripto Indonesia as a digital financial asset trader (effective 2026-09-22) completed its processing of all applications from prospective crypto traders handed over by Bappebti, the commodity futures regulator. OJK counts 34 licensed crypto entities: 2 exchanges, 2 clearing institutions, 2 custodians and 28 traders. [OJK — Siaran Pers: Stabilitas Sektor Jasa Keuangan Terjaga di Tengah Meningkatnya Tekanan Perekonomian Global (Financial services sector stability maintained amid rising global economic pressure), SP 185/OJK/DKPU/X/2026, 2026-10-05](https://ojk.go.id/id/berita-dan-kegiatan/siaran-pers/Pages/RDKB-September-2026.aspx) → [lesson 1.4](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-4)
- **2026-10-07 · India · open finance (Account Aggregators) · announced.** The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) said non-bank Account Aggregators will be made interoperable, so customers can share data from any financial information provider through the aggregator of their choice, and depositories will be able to include bank-deposit information in consolidated account statements through aggregators. Both are expected to be implemented by 2026-12-31; implementing circulars were not found in the period. [RBI — Statement on Developmental and Regulatory Policies October 7, 2026 (Press Release 2026-2027/1265), 2026-10-07](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63743) → [lesson 2.3](../Textbook/02-How-a-payment-works/README.md#module-2-3)
- **2026-10-07 · Singapore · AI risk management, including agentic AI · final guidelines issued.** The Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) issued final Guidelines on Artificial Intelligence Risk Management that apply to all financial institutions and all forms of AI, taking effect on 2027-10-07: the expectations in sections 3–4 apply from that date, and those in sections 5–6 must be met by 2028-10-07. MAS cites the rise of agentic AI systems that can act autonomously and plans to consult in 2027 on further agentic-AI guidance; the release does not address agent-initiated payments specifically. [MAS — MAS Sets Out Supervisory Expectations on Responsible AI Adoption by Financial Institutions, 2026-10-07](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/mas-sets-out-supervisory-expectations-on-responsible-ai-adoption-by-financial-institutions) → [lesson 7.5](../Textbook/07-AI-agent-payments/README.md#module-7-5)
- **2026-10-09 · Singapore · wholesale CBDC · second trial issuance.** MAS issued a second tokenized MAS Bill (TD26101Z, S$10 million offered), auctioned on 2026-10-07 and again settled in wholesale central bank digital currency (CBDC) on the Singapore dollar test network. The first issue was on 2026-09-23 (week 39). [MAS — Tokenised MAS Bill: auctions and issuance calendar, updated 2026-10-06](https://www.mas.gov.sg/bonds-and-bills/auctions-and-issuance-calendar/mas-bills-type-tokenised-mas-bill) → [lesson 6.8](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-8)
- **2026-10-09 · Malaysia · basic credit cards · announced.** Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM) said in its Budget 2027 statement that banks will start offering "basic credit cards" in phases from October 2026: no-frills cards without rewards or cashback, with interest or profit capped at 14% a year, to which existing cardholders may switch. Launch dates by bank and the underlying policy document were not checked. [BNM — Belanjawan 2027 (Budget 2027), 2026-10-09](https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/belanjawan2027) → [lesson 5.5](../Textbook/05-Wallets-accounts-and-cards/README.md#module-5-5)

## Middle East and Türkiye

- **2026-10-06 · UAE (Dubai) · audits of crypto reserve assets · circular issued.** Dubai's Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (VARA) issued a circular setting minimum expectations for independent audits of virtual asset service providers' reserve assets, after reviewing all proof-of-reserve reports submitted in 2025. It restates that reserves must cover 100% of client liabilities, held 1:1 in the same virtual asset, reconciled daily and independently audited, and sets a seven-point minimum audit scope covering all wallets and third-party custodians; it gives no effective date. [VARA — Circular: Reserve Assets Audit Report, 2026-10-06](https://media.umbraco.io/dwtc/p1zdm1ig/circular-regarding-reserve-assets-audit-report.pdf) → [lesson 6.3](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-3)
- **2026-10-06 · UAE (Abu Dhabi Global Market) · DeFi risk management · consultation.** The Financial Services Regulatory Authority (FSRA) of Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) proposed guidance on how authorized firms and recognized bodies should manage risks from engaging with decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols and venues, covering governance, due diligence, monitoring, financial-crime controls and client-asset safeguards. Comments close on 2026-11-30. [ADGM — ADGM FSRA Invites Industry Feedback on Proposed DeFi Risk Management Guidance, 2026-10-06](https://www.adgm.com/media/announcements/adgm-fsra-invites-industry-feedback-on-proposed-defi-risk-management-guidance) → [lesson 6.5](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-5)
- **2026-10-07 · Türkiye · customer identification thresholds and remote verification · in force.** A presidential decision amending Türkiye's regulation on anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing measures doubled customer identification thresholds from 185,000 to 370,000 Turkish lira and from 15,000 to 30,000 lira, raised the 15,000-lira thresholds in Articles 24 and 24/A to 30,000 lira, and lets financial institutions skip a signature specimen when identity is verified through internet or mobile channels or by a one-time SMS password to a verified phone number. It took effect on publication. [Resmî Gazete No. 33393 — Suç Gelirlerinin Aklanmasının ve Terörün Finansmanının Önlenmesine Dair Tedbirler Hakkında Yönetmelikte Değişiklik Yapılmasına Dair Yönetmelik (Regulation amending the AML/CFT Measures Regulation), Decision No. 11845, 2026-10-07](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2026/10/20261007-9.pdf) → [lesson 1.5](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-5)

## Africa

- **2026-10-08 · West African Economic and Monetary Union (WAEMU) · government payments on the instant platform · launched (one treasury live).** The Central Bank of West African States (BCEAO) announced that public treasuries are connecting to PI-SPI, the union's instant payment platform, to send and receive payments such as salaries, grants, taxes and fees with real-time tracking. Côte d'Ivoire's treasury already makes bulk payments through it; the other treasuries are being integrated step by step. [BCEAO — « Connexion des Trésors Publics à la Plateforme Interopérable du Système de Paiement Instantané (PI-SPI)… » (Connection of public treasuries to the interoperable instant payment platform PI-SPI), 2026-10-08](https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-des-tresors-publics-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement) → [lesson 2.3](../Textbook/02-How-a-payment-works/README.md#module-2-3)

## Russia and CIS

- **2026-10-06 · Russia · crypto custody and exchange · first firms admitted.** The Bank of Russia entered the first 5 organizations in its register of digital depositories and 4 in its register of crypto exchangers, under the transitional provisions of the law on digital currencies and digital rights, in force since 2026-09-01. Digital depositories may record and transfer digital currencies and digital rights and give clients access to address identifiers, crypto exchangers may buy and sell digital currencies in their own name and on their own account outside organized trading, and all must bring their activities fully in line with the law by 2027-09-01. [Банк России — «Банк России допустил первые цифровые депозитарии и криптообменники к работе на финансовом рынке» (Bank of Russia admitted the first digital depositories and crypto exchangers to the financial market), 2026-10-06](https://www.cbr.ru/press/event/?id=32894) → [lesson 1.4](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-4), [lesson 5.3](../Textbook/05-Wallets-accounts-and-cards/README.md#module-5-3)
- **2026-10-09 · Kazakhstan · digital financial asset issuance · draft (consultation).** The National Bank of Kazakhstan and the Agency for Regulation and Development of the Financial Market published a draft joint resolution setting conditions for Kazakh banks and other legal entities to issue digital financial assets in the Astana International Financial Centre (AIFC), and for AIFC participants to issue them on platforms outside the AIFC. It is a draft, and the release gives no adoption or effective date. [Национальный Банк Казахстана — «Нацбанк и АРРФР разработали проект совместного постановления Правления по взаимному допуску эмитентов цифровых финансовых активов» (The National Bank and the financial market regulator developed a draft joint Board resolution on mutual admission of issuers of digital financial assets), 2026-10-09](https://nationalbank.kz/ru/news/informacionnye-soobshcheniya/20438) → [lesson 1.4](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-4)
- **2026-10-10 · Georgia · crypto provider anti-money laundering · fine.** The National Bank of Georgia (NBG) fined virtual asset service provider Bitexchange LLC a total of GEL 572,118,000, almost all of it for transactions through self-service kiosks without identifying and verifying customers by the procedure agreed with the NBG; it also cited weaknesses in sanctions, politically exposed person and suspicious-transaction screening. The statement does not give the date the fine was imposed. [National Bank of Georgia — National Bank of Georgia Statement on the Fine Imposed on Bitexchange LLC, 2026-10-10](https://nbg.gov.ge/en/media/news/national-bank-of-georgia-statement-on-the-fine-imposed-on-bitexchange-llc) → [lesson 1.5](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-5)
