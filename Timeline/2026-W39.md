# Week 39 · 21–27 September 2026

Events dated 2026-09-21 … 2026-09-27 by their primary source. Researched 2026-10-22; every entry was checked against its primary source by a second agent. Teaching edition with limitations — not legal advice.

## Highlights

- The Eurosystem launched Pontes, which settles wholesale transactions in tokenized assets in central bank money.
- The US Federal Reserve Board proposed rules for the payment stablecoin issuers it supervises under the GENIUS Act.
- Brazil moved the start of its ban on banks dealing with unauthorized crypto providers to 2026-11-06 and, by provisional measure, ordered payment providers to block transactions to fixed-odds betting.
- The Monetary Authority of Singapore issued its first tokenized MAS Bill, settled in wholesale central bank digital currency on a test network.
- SoFi and Mastercard said stablecoin settlement is live for SoFi Bank's card program.

## EU and UK

- **2026-09-21 · EU · wholesale settlement of tokenized assets · launched.** The Eurosystem launched Pontes, which settles wholesale transactions in tokenized assets in central bank money by linking distributed ledger technology (DLT) platforms with the Eurosystem's TARGET Services; 13 initial participants, the Bundesbank and four DLT operators joined at launch. Enhanced features and longer operating hours will come gradually, with full implementation expected by 2028; this is wholesale settlement, not a retail central bank digital currency (CBDC). [ECB — Eurosystem brings central bank money to tokenised finance, 2026-09-21](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2026/html/ecb.pr260921~e754847a7b.en.html); [ECB — Pontes](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/paym/target/pontes/html/index.en.html) → [lesson 6.8](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-8)
- **2026-09-24 · EU · cross-border instant payments · feasibility assessment approved.** The Governing Council of the European Central Bank (ECB) approved an assessment, with the Banco Central do Brasil, of linking the Eurosystem's TARGET Instant Payment Settlement (TIPS) with Brazil's Pix — a feasibility study, not a decision to build. The ECB also lists its joining of Nexus Global Payments and planned links with India's Unified Payments Interface (UPI) and Switzerland's SIC instant payment system, without dates. [ECB — ECB to assess feasibility of interlinking with Brazil's instant payment system Pix, 2026-09-24](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/intro/news/html/ecb.mipnews260924.en.html) → [lesson 2.3](../Textbook/02-How-a-payment-works/README.md#module-2-3)

## United States and Canada

- **2026-09-24 · United States · stablecoin issuers (GENIUS Act) · proposed.** The Federal Reserve Board proposed rules for the payment stablecoin issuers it supervises, covering reserve assets, capital, risk management, a ban on paying yield or interest (including indirectly through third parties) and an anti-tying rule for all permitted issuers, plus a separate application procedure for bank subsidiaries that want to issue stablecoins. Both proposals were published in the Federal Register on 2026-09-29, with comments due 2026-11-30. [Federal Reserve Board — Federal Reserve Board requests public comment on two proposals related to establishing a regulatory framework for Board-supervised payment stablecoin issuers under the GENIUS Act, 2026-09-24](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20260924a.htm); [Federal Register 2026-19860 — Implementing the Federal Reserve Board's Responsibilities Under the GENIUS Act, 2026-09-29](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-09-29/html/2026-19860.htm); [Federal Register 2026-19899 — Application Procedures for Board-Supervised Insured Depository Institutions Seeking Approval for a Subsidiary To Issue Payment Stablecoins, 2026-09-29](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/29/2026-19899/application-procedures-for-board-supervised-insured-depository-institutions-seeking-approval-for-a) → [lesson 1.4](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-4), [lesson 6.3](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-3)

## Latin America

- **2026-09-23 · Brazil · crypto service providers · rule amended (adopted).** Resolution 589 of the Central Bank of Brazil (BCB) amended the rules for virtual asset service providers (Resolution 520): the ban on banks and other BCB-authorized institutions operating with unauthorized crypto providers now starts on 2026-11-06 instead of 2026-10-30, and its new wording no longer mentions providers "in the authorization process". It was published in the official gazette on 2026-09-25; the change to this article is effective from 2026-10-01, and new reporting duties for applicants (client balances, proof of reserves, staking) apply from 2027-01-01. [BCB — Resolução BCB nº 589, de 23 de setembro de 2026 (BCB Resolution No. 589 of 23 September 2026), 2026-09-23](https://www.bcb.gov.br/api/conteudo/app/normativos/exibenormativo?p1=Resolu%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20BCB&p2=589); [BCB — Resolução BCB nº 520, texto consolidado (BCB Resolution No. 520, consolidated text)](https://www.bcb.gov.br/api/conteudo/app/normativos/exibenormativo?p1=Resolu%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20BCB&p2=520) → [lesson 1.4](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-4)
- **2026-09-25 · Brazil · betting ban enforced through payments · provisional measure in force.** Provisional Measure 1.394 banned fixed-odds betting nationwide and bars financial and payment institutions and payment-arrangement owners and participants from processing, settling or enabling transactions to such betting, including instant payments, except wind-down and refund flows. It applies from publication but must be converted into law by Congress, and it tells the BCB to build a system for rejecting such real-time transfers and returning funds. [Senado Federal — Medida Provisória nº 1.394, de 25 de setembro de 2026 (Provisional Measure No. 1,394 of 25 September 2026), 2026-09-25](https://legis.senado.leg.br/norma/44059160/publicacao/44061634) → [lesson 3.6](../Textbook/03-Visa-and-Mastercard-cards/README.md#module-3-6), [lesson 2.3](../Textbook/02-How-a-payment-works/README.md#module-2-3)

## Asia-Pacific

- **2026-09-21 · Philippines · digital bank licensing · circular issued.** Circular No. 1240 of Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) requires thrift, rural and cooperative banks that BSP finds operating like digital banks to meet the ₱1.0 billion minimum capital of digital banks within six months of BSP's notice, and allows BSP to issue more digital bank licenses, including by converting such banks. BSP announced the circular on 2026-09-23 and counts seven licensed digital banks. [BSP — BSP issues capital, other requirements for banks shifting to digital model, 2026-09-23](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/SitePages/MediaAndResearch/MediaDisp.aspx?ItemId=8084&MType=MediaReleases) → [lesson 1.2](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-2)
- **2026-09-22 · Taiwan · tokenized deposits · pilot applications opened.** Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC) opened the way for banks to apply to pilot "deposit token" business, stating that a deposit token's legal nature is still a bank deposit governed by the Banking Act. Applicants must describe use cases, issuance-to-redemption flows, reconciliation between the deposit and token ledgers, the technical architecture and customer protection; the release only opens applications and names no approved bank. [FSC — 金管會開放銀行申請試辦存款代幣業務 (FSC opens bank applications to pilot deposit-token business), 2026-09-22](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0,2&mcustomize=news_view.jsp&dataserno=202609220001&dtable=News) → [lesson 6.1](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-1)

[MAS — Tokenised MAS Bill: auctions and issuance calendar, updated 2026-10-06](https://www.mas.gov.sg/bonds-and-bills/auctions-and-issuance-calendar/mas-bills-type-tokenised-mas-bill) → [lesson 6.8](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-8)

## Middle East and Türkiye

- **2026-09-23 · UAE · anti-money laundering enforcement against a bank · enforcement.** The Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) imposed enforcement measures on the UAE branches of Bank Melli Iran for violations including anti-money laundering, counter-terrorist financing and proliferation-financing obligations, under Article 168-1-C of the new central bank law (Federal Decree-Law No. 6 of 2025). All its UAE branches are barred from any financial transactions to and from Iran, including trade finance and fund transfers; no fine amount or duration was stated. [CBUAE — The Central Bank imposes strict sanctions on the branches of Bank Melli Iran operating in the United Arab Emirates, 2026-09-23](https://centralbank.ae/media/vq0htmbi/the-central-bank-imposes-strict-sanctions-on-the-branches-of-bank-melli-iran-operating-in-the-united-arab-emirates.pdf) → [lesson 1.5](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-5)
- **2026-09-23 · Türkiye · customer due diligence thresholds · in force.** Communiqué No. 33 of Türkiye's Financial Crimes Investigation Board (MASAK) raised several Turkish lira thresholds in its General Communiqué No. 5 — for example from 10,000 to 20,000 TL, from 25,000 to 50,000 TL and from 2,750 to 5,500 TL — and Communiqué No. 34 requires immediate measures for persons listed by UN Security Council decisions; both took effect on publication. Which payment instruments each threshold covers was not checked in the primary text. [Resmî Gazete No. 33379 — Mali Suçları Araştırma Kurulu Genel Tebliği (Sıra No: 5)'nde Değişiklik Yapılmasına Dair Tebliğ (Sıra No: 33) (Communiqué No. 33 amending MASAK General Communiqué No. 5), 2026-09-23](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2026/09/20260923-3.htm); [Resmî Gazete No. 33379 — Mali Suçları Araştırma Kurulu Genel Tebliği (Sıra No: 34) (MASAK General Communiqué No. 34), 2026-09-23](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2026/09/20260923-2.htm) → [lesson 1.5](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-5)

## Africa

- **2026-09-21 · Kenya · payments law · consultation opened.** Kenya's National Treasury and the Central Bank of Kenya published for comment a draft National Payment System Policy and the National Payment System Bill, 2026, which would repeal the current National Payment System Act; the bill's clauses include interoperability, open finance, agents, funds held in trust accounts and cross-border payments. Comments were due by 2026-10-09; it is a draft, not law. [Central Bank of Kenya — Draft National Payment System Policy and National Payment System Bill, 2026, 2026-09-21](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/2026/09/21/draft-national-payment-system-policy-and-national-payment-system-bill-2026/) → [lesson 1.4](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-4), [lesson 2.3](../Textbook/02-How-a-payment-works/README.md#module-2-3)

## Russia and CIS

- **2026-09-24 · Russia · crypto market admission · adopted; applies from 2026-10-05.** The Bank of Russia set the admission procedure for its crypto market: digital depositories (custody and record-keeping), crypto exchangers and operators of information systems that issue digital rights must each be entered in a Bank of Russia register, with fit-and-proper requirements for management and a decision procedure that includes simplified admission. Both acts are registered with the Ministry of Justice and take effect on 2026-10-05. [Банк России — «Как попасть в реестр Банка России для работы на рынке криптовалют» (How to get onto the Bank of Russia register to work on the cryptocurrency market), 2026-09-24](https://www.cbr.ru/press/event/?id=32872) → [lesson 1.4](../Textbook/01-Market-rules-and-licensing/README.md#module-1-4), [lesson 6.5](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-5)

## International bodies

- **2026-09-23 · Financial Action Task Force · mutual evaluation of Türkiye · published.** The Financial Action Task Force (FATF) published its 2026 Mutual Evaluation Report of Türkiye, which finds a comprehensive licensing framework for financial institutions and says a "solid regulatory framework was still being implemented" for virtual asset service providers at the time of the on-site visit. ## Global networks and AI-agent payments

- **2026-09-21 · Swift · ISO 20022 standards release · new date set.** Swift set 2027-06-12 as the go-live date of its deferred Standards Release 2026, replacing "Q1 2027"; the release covers MT messages other than Category 1 and several groups of ISO 20022 messages. Swift's earlier deferral of 2026-08-27, before the cutoff, also postponed the structured postal address requirement for ISO 20022 payment messages that was due in November 2026, and Swift will announce new timing "by December, at the latest". [Swift — Swift accepts community request to extend structured address migration for ISO 20022 payment messages, updated 2026-09-21](https://www.swift.com/insights/news/swift-accepts-community-request-extend-structured-address-migration-iso-20022-payment-messages) → [lesson 2.3](../Textbook/02-How-a-payment-works/README.md#module-2-3)

SoFi says it is moving its whole card program (about $25 billion of annual volume) to stablecoin settlement; "live" is the companies' own claim, and the settlement mechanics are not described. [SoFi Technologies — SoFi Becomes First National Bank to Go Live with Stablecoin Settlement across Mastercard's Global Payments Network, 2026-09-22](https://investors.sofi.com/news/news-details/2026/SoFi-Becomes-First-National-Bank-to-Go-Live-with-Stablecoin-Settlement-across-Mastercards-Global-Payments-Network/default.aspx) → [lesson 3.3](../Textbook/03-Visa-and-Mastercard-cards/README.md#module-3-3), [lesson 6.3](../Textbook/06-Crypto-and-stablecoins/README.md#module-6-3)

On 2026-09-24 Block announced that it had joined the x402 Foundation and contributed Lightning payments to the protocol. [x402-foundation/x402 — commit 6fe0d4bf "Specify exact Lightning on lnbtc (#2861)", 2026-09-23](https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402/commit/6fe0d4bfd104e8c61ae0b6aeaefe9da506d502ff); [Block — Block Joins the x402 Foundation to Advance Open, Agentic Commerce, 2026-09-24](https://block.xyz/inside/block-joins-the-x402-foundation-to-advance-open-agentic-commerce) → [lesson 7.3](../Textbook/07-AI-agent-payments/README.md#module-7-3)
