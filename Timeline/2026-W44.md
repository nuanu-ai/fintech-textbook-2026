# Week 44 · 26 October – 1 November 2026

Events dated 2026-10-26 … 2026-11-01 by their primary source. Researched 2026-11-09 and checked against primary sources in a separate pass on 2026-11-10. Teaching edition with limitations — not legal advice.

## Highlights

- The Council of the EU adopted its first-reading positions on PSD3 and the Payment Services Regulation.
- Online Wero payments went live at participating Dutch banks, with merchants to follow in phases.
- The EPC will revisit the end date for unstructured addresses in February 2027.
- FATF launched a targeted review of how jurisdictions with significant virtual-asset activity apply its standards.
