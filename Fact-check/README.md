## What the verdicts mean

| Verdict | Records | How to read it |
|---|---:|---|
| `verified` | 296 | Confirmed within the checked source and wording. |
| `verified_as_reported` | 78 | Confirmed that the source reports this; there is no independent reproduction. |
| `partial` | 24 | The source partly confirms the claim. |
| `unverifiable` | 21 | There is not enough available evidence to check it. |
| `non_factual` | 38 | A teaching model, hypothesis or editorial position; not an established external fact. |
| `contradicted` | 14 | Refuted; this includes myths that the textbook itself examines and rejects. |

These are the results of the public edition. The existence of a fact-check does not mean that every statement is fully proven or remains current over time.

## How the check was done

Ten agents prepared the chapters; five others carried out the first review. In the second pass, three new auditors went through every record and source. The editor fixed the findings, and the auditors re-checked the changes. The detailed fact-check was completed on **17 September 2026, 17:08 WITA**. The public adaptation and layout of 18 September are not a new external fact-check.
